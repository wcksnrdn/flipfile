Changes made for flipfile: the released weights (4xNomos2_realplksr_dysample.safetensors)
were converted to Core ML (upscale-photo.mlmodelc) and ONNX (upscale-photo.onnx) for
256 px tiles, with the weights stored in float16; the network (RealPLKSR with a DySample
upsampler) was reimplemented for this in scripts/upscale-model/realplksr.py. The weights
are provided as is, without warranties of any kind.
