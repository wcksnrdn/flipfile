Changes made for flipfile: the released weights (4xFaceUpSharpLDAT.safetensors) were
converted to Core ML (faces-upscale.mlmodelc) and ONNX (faces-upscale.onnx) for 128 px
tiles, with the weights stored as 8-bit integers; the network (DAT, Chen et al., ICCV
2023) was reimplemented for this in scripts/upscale-model/dat.py. The weights are
provided as is, without warranties of any kind.
