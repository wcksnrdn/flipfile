Changes made for flipfile: the released weights (4xNomos8kSC.safetensors, Real-ESRGAN's
RRDBNet trained further by Philip Hofmann on his Nomos8k dataset with JPEG compression
and slight blur) were blended 30% with 70% of Real-ESRGAN x4plus's weights (network
interpolation; x4plus is BSD-3-Clause, see LICENSE-Real-ESRGAN.txt), and the result
converted to Core ML (upscale-photo.mlmodelc) and ONNX (upscale-photo.onnx) for 256 px
tiles, with the weights stored in float16; the network is reimplemented in
scripts/upscale-model/rrdbnet.py. The weights are provided as is,
without warranties of any kind.
