words.txt: the Indonesian and English words Upscale Text accepts as real words on Windows
and Linux (macOS uses its own spelling dictionaries).

Made from the word frequency lists of the Leipzig Corpora Collection, corpora
ind_mixed_2013_1M (Indonesian, mixed sources) and eng_news_2024_1M (English, news):
https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download

D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart, U. Quasthoff: "Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the
Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages", Proceedings of LREC 2012.

Changes made for flipfile: only words made of letters and used at least three times
were kept, lowercased, without their counts, and the two lists merged and sorted.
